Notes for GLM-4.7-Flash

  • Weights: the BF16 checkpoint zai-org/GLM-4.7-Flash (revision 7dd20894), converted to Megatron torch_dist with miles' tools/convert_hf_to_torch_dist.py at miles 0.1.0 (commit 78527b91).
  • MODEL_ARGS come from miles' scripts/models/glm4.7-flash.py at commit 78527b91, printed by miles/utils/external_utils/model_args_utils.py glm4.7-flash; run the command from a miles checkout at that commit.
  • It trains on its own rows, built with --loss-mask-type glm5 after data/patch_glm5_mask.py (data/README.md). Its tokenizer.json is GLM-5.2's, but its chat template differs, so the GLM-5.2 rows do not apply.

#1attune-tp1-pp1-ep86,178.5 tokens/s/GPUTP1 PP1 EP8 DP8max 16,384 tokens/GPU, no offload
  • Starting point: miles' GLM-4.7-Flash launcher, scripts/run_glm47_flash.py at miles 0.1.0 (model page https://miles.radixark.com/docs/models/glm/glm4-7-flash). miles publishes it as a GRPO recipe with no tokens/s figure. Ported to this workload with its layout (TP 4 with sequence parallel, EP 8, full recompute, max 32,768 tokens/GPU, Adam on the CPU) on 8 H200, it measured 3,142.9 tokens/s/GPU; ATTUNE tuned it to 6,178.5 (+97%).
  • The loss starts 1.0% above the reference recipe's (1.1580 against 1.1465 at step 0; Hugging Face transformers gives 1.1536 on the same 128 rows). In steps 0-3, where both recipes' gradient norms run at 16-276 before clipping to 1.0, it rises to 1.34 at step 3 against the reference's 0.97; from step 6 it stays within 1.2% of the reference and from step 12 within 0.5%.

  --actor-num-nodes 1 \
  --actor-num-gpus-per-node 8 \

  --hf-checkpoint "$HF_CKPT" \
  --ref-load "$TORCH_DIST" \

  --input-key messages \
  --metadata-key metadata \
  --num-rollout 20 \
  --rollout-batch-size 128 \
  --global-batch-size 128 \
  --tensor-model-parallel-size 1 \
  --pipeline-model-parallel-size 1 \
  --context-parallel-size 1 \
  --expert-model-parallel-size 8 \
  --expert-tensor-parallel-size 1 \
  --recompute-granularity full \
  --recompute-method uniform \
  --recompute-num-layers 1 \
  --use-dynamic-batch-size \
  --max-tokens-per-gpu 16384 \
  --use-precision-aware-optimizer \
  --attention-backend flash \
  --optimizer adam --lr 1e-5 --lr-decay-style cosine --min-lr 1e-6 \
  --lr-warmup-fraction 0.1 --weight-decay 0.1 --adam-beta1 0.9 --adam-beta2 0.98 \
  --attention-dropout 0.0 --hidden-dropout 0.0 \
  --accumulate-allreduce-grads-in-fp32 --attention-softmax-in-fp32 \
  --distributed-timeout-minutes 30
