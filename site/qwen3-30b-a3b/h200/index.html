Notes for Qwen3-30B-A3B

  • Weights: the BF16 checkpoint Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B (revision ad44e777), converted to Megatron torch_dist with miles' tools/convert_hf_to_torch_dist.py at commit 78527b91; --ref-load is the conversion and --hf-checkpoint the checkpoint.
  • MODEL_ARGS come from miles' scripts/models/qwen3-30B-A3B.py at commit 78527b91, printed by miles/utils/external_utils/model_args_utils.py qwen3-30B-A3B; run the command from a miles checkout at that commit.
  • It trains on the Qwen3-32B rows, built with slime's qwen3 loss mask: its tokenizer.json, tokenizer_config.json (which holds the chat template), vocab.json and merges.txt are byte-identical to Qwen3-32B's (data/README.md).

#1attune-tp1-pp1-ep8-mt16k6,913.2 tokens/s/GPUTP1 PP1 EP8 DP8max 16,384 tokens/GPU, no offload, allocator without expandable segments
  • Starting point: miles' Qwen3-30B-A3B launcher, scripts/run_qwen3_30b_a3b.py at miles 0.1.0 (model page https://miles.radixark.com/docs/models/qwen/qwen3-moe). miles publishes it as a GRPO recipe with no tokens/s figure. Ported to this workload with its one-node layout (TP 4 with sequence parallel, EP 8, full recompute, max 32,768 tokens/GPU, Adam on the CPU) on 8 H200, it measured 3,129.7 tokens/s/GPU; ATTUNE tuned it to 6,913.2 (+121%).
  • miles empties the CUDA cache after every step (train.py calls clear_memory, which runs torch.cuda.empty_cache), so the caching allocator acquires its memory again each step. With PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:False the step took 19.0 s, against 20.8 s with expandable segments in an otherwise identical tuning run (6,296.3 tokens/s/GPU).
  • Keep the full recompute: tuning runs with recompute off and with selective recompute ran out of memory at step 0 at two rows per microbatch.
