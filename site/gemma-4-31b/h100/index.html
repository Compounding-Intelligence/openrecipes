Notes for Gemma-4-31B

  • Weights: the BF16 checkpoint google/gemma-4-31B-it (revision 842da379), with no conversion: miles loads it through Megatron-Bridge (--megatron-to-hf-mode bridge), so --hf-checkpoint and --ref-load are both the checkpoint directory.
  • MODEL_ARGS come from miles' scripts/models/gemma-4-31b-it.py at commit 78527b91, printed by miles/utils/external_utils/model_args_utils.py gemma-4-31b-it; run the command from a miles checkout at that commit, in the radixark/miles v0.1.0 image, whose Megatron-Bridge (commit 7f0fb345) holds the dense Gemma 4 provider the launcher requires.
  • It trains on the Gemma 4 rows: its tokenizer.json, tokenizer_config.json and chat_template.jinja are byte-identical to Gemma-4-26B-A4B's (data/README.md).
  • Selective recompute and --micro-batch-size 1 replace the launcher's full recompute and dynamic batching, as for Gemma-4-26B-A4B, and --max-tokens-per-gpu has no effect without dynamic batching.
  • On H100, --attention-backend auto runs cuDNN fused attention on the 50 sliding-window layers and TE's unfused attention on the 10 global layers (head dim 512).

#1attune-tp2-pp4-split131,178.1 tokens/s/GPUTP2 PP4 DP113/16/16/15 layer split, CPU optimizer offload 0.8, cuDNN fused sliding-window attention
  • Starting point: miles' Gemma-4-31B launcher, scripts/run_gemma_4_31b.py at miles 0.1.0 (model page https://miles.radixark.com/docs/models/gemma/gemma-4). miles publishes it as a GRPO recipe for 8 H200 with no tokens/s figure. Ported to this workload with its TP 4 with sequence parallel, TE's unfused attention and Adam on the CPU on 8 H100, with PP 2, selective recompute of the core attention and the MLP and one row per microbatch, it measured 743.4 tokens/s/GPU; ATTUNE tuned it to 1,178.1 (+58%).
  • --optimizer-offload-fraction 0.8 keeps 20% of the optimizer state on the GPU and shortens the CPU Adam step. At 0.8 the first pipeline stage runs with less than 10% of its memory free.
  • At step 0 the loss on the first 128 rows is 2.27393, against 2.26972 for the reference recipe; Hugging Face transformers 5.12.1 gives 2.27153 on the same rows.
  • The convergence check covers 20 steps. This recipe runs cuDNN fused attention on the sliding-window layers, where the reference recipe runs TE's unfused attention, and on another MoE model cuDNN fused attention passed the 20-step check while its loss drifted up later (ATTUNE's lever catalog). Compare the loss over a longer run before a full training run.
