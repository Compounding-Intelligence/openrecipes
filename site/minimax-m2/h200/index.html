Notes for MiniMax-M2

  • Weights: the FP8 checkpoint MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2 (revision 757303d4), cast to BF16 with slime's tools/fp8_cast_bf16.py and converted to Megatron torch_dist with slime's tools/convert_hf_to_torch_dist.py and the recipes' MODEL_ARGS on one 8-GPU node. The BF16 directory also holds the checkout's tokenizer files, chat_template.jinja, generation_config.json, the remote-code files configuration_minimax_m2.py and modeling_minimax_m2.py, and its config.json without quantization_config. --hf-checkpoint is the BF16 directory and --ref-load the conversion.
  • The conversion needs one edit to slime 0.3.0 on Megatron-LM commit 1dcf0daf: in slime_plugins/mbridge/minimax_m2.py, MiniMaxM2Bridge._build_config passes rotary_percent=0.5 to _build_base_config, which hands it to Megatron's TransformerConfig. That class has no rotary_percent field, so the conversion stops with a TypeError. Delete that keyword argument before converting. The bridge's config only maps the weights; training builds the model from --rotary-percent 0.5 in MODEL_ARGS.
  • MODEL_ARGS come from slime's scripts/models/minimax-m2.sh at slime 0.3.0. They select slime's MiniMax-M2 layer spec (--spec slime_plugins.models.minimax_m2 get_minimax_m2_layer_spec) and the alltoall dispatcher.
  • It trains on its own rows, built with --loss-mask-type minimax_m2 (data/README.md).
  • Load the checkpoint's config and tokenizer once on each node before the training processes start (AutoConfig.from_pretrained and AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained on the BF16 directory with trust_remote_code=True). Otherwise the processes of a node import the remote-code module configuration_minimax_m2 at the same time, and one can stop with an AttributeError for MiniMaxM2Config.

#1attune-tp1-pp8-ep8-vpp42,034.4 tokens/s/GPUTP1 PP8 VPP4 EP8 DP8VPP 4, no offload
  • Starting point: slime's MiniMax-M2 script, scripts/run-minimax-m2.sh with scripts/models/minimax-m2.sh at slime 0.3.0. slime ships it as a 16-node GRPO script that shares the GPUs with its rollout engine and publishes no tokens/s figure for it. Ported to this workload on 64 H200 with its layout (TP 2 with sequence parallel, PP 2, full recompute, Adam on the CPU) and EP 8 in place of its EP 4, at which each GPU's expert weights and FP32 gradients alone exceed its memory, it measured 557.8 tokens/s/GPU; ATTUNE tuned it to 2,034.4 (+265%).
  • --pipeline-model-parallel-layout 'Et|(tt|)*30tL' cuts the 62 layers into 32 virtual stages, 4 per pipeline rank: the first holds the embedding and 1 layer, the last 1 layer and the loss, and the 30 in between 2 layers each, so ranks 0 and 7 hold 7 layers and the others 8.
  • The reference recipe runs at TP 2 with sequence parallelism, where slime's MiniMax-M2 layer spec gives the full-width q and k norm weights twice their gradient: each TP rank applies the norm to the gathered q and k, and Megatron sums the gradients of these sequence-parallel weights over TP. The forward pass is unchanged, and this recipe, at TP 1, is not affected.
