Notes for Gemma-4-26B-A4B

  • Weights: the BF16 checkpoint google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it (revision 4d7ae498), with no conversion: miles loads it through Megatron-Bridge (--megatron-to-hf-mode bridge), so --hf-checkpoint and --ref-load are both the checkpoint directory.
  • MODEL_ARGS come from miles' scripts/models/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it.py at commit 78527b91, printed by miles/utils/external_utils/model_args_utils.py gemma-4-26b-a4b-it; run the command from a miles checkout at that commit, in the radixark/miles v0.1.0 image, whose Megatron-Bridge (commit 7f0fb345) holds the Gemma 4 provider.
  • It trains on the Gemma 4 rows, built with the gemma4 loss mask (data/README.md).
  • Selective recompute and --micro-batch-size 1 replace the launcher's full recompute and dynamic batching: full recompute stops at step 0, where save_for_backward receives Gemma 4's rotary embedding as a tuple, and miles refuses dynamic batching with --qkv-format bshd. --max-tokens-per-gpu stays from the launcher and has no effect without dynamic batching. Of the selective recompute modules, moe_act and layernorm stop in the first backward.
  • On B200, keep --attention-backend unfused. With auto, TE 2.17 selects FlashAttention 4 for the sliding-window layers, whose sm100 kernel does not support a local window at head dim 256, and the run stops at step 0; with fused, no cuDNN backend covers the layers. On H200, auto runs cuDNN fused attention on the 25 sliding-window layers and TE's unfused attention on the 5 global layers (head dim 512).

  • At step 0 the loss on the first 128 rows is 4.28136, against 4.28294 for the reference recipe; Hugging Face transformers 5.12.1 gives 4.28956 on the same rows.
